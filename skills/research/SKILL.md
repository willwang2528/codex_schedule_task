# Research

Use this workflow when a task asks for a research brief, literature scan, or evidence-backed Top K.

## Outcome

Produce a candidate pool wider than the final output. Follow the task's scope and date window; do not silently narrow the question to one convenient keyword.

## Workflow

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, the task YAML, task prompt, and prior state.
2. Expand the topic into direct terms, neighboring mechanisms, benchmarks, venues, organizations, and update signals.
3. Search several distinct queries and source lanes.
4. Record every viable candidate with title, authors, organization, date, source, URL, publication status, topic, and claimed contribution.
5. Send serious candidates to source verification. Community pages remain discovery signals only.

Do not manufacture candidates to meet a numeric target. If the pool is smaller because high-quality new work is scarce, state that fact.
