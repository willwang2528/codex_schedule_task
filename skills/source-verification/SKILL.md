# Source Verification

Use this workflow after discovery and before ranking.

## Source order

Prefer the paper or official proceedings record, then the official project or repository, then authoritative metadata. Community summaries may help locate sources but cannot substantiate the final claim.

## Verification record

For each serious candidate, establish:

- canonical title, authors, date, URL, and publication status;
- whether it is published, a preprint, under review, a technical report, a benchmark, or code;
- the actual method-level contribution, not promotional wording;
- direct relevance to the task rather than keyword overlap;
- whether experiments and benchmarks support the claimed contribution;
- whether the item duplicates prior work or is only a minor release.

Check the abstract, introduction, method, experiments, conclusion, and official repository as needed. Reject a candidate when no primary source exists or the evidence does not support its claim. Preserve uncertainty explicitly rather than guessing.
